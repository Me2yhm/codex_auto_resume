## Phase 3 — same-host recovery priority and Primer dispatch safety

- Per-controller atomic recovery leases publish pending/armed/verifying across workspace windows; heartbeat is independent of quota polling. Expired leases plus grace are ignored; malformed/unreadable coordination suppresses Primer while Auto Resume continues.
- Primer checks quota, active work (including pagination), and recovery priority at candidate, post-jitter, locked preflight, and immediately before dispatch. Manual Prime uses the same protection. A fresh motion interval outside the lock detects windows activated by real work after the original candidate.
- A host-authoritative atomic send journal survives independent Memento caches and restarts. Uncertain dispatch remains pending without expiry. Accepted dispatch releases the lock before completion observation; definitive unsent cancellation clears its intent.
- Lock ownership tokens, live-process checks, and stale-lock retirement fences prevent delayed holders/creators from deleting or overwriting a successor. Tiny retired lock directories are retained as fencing evidence.
- Official installed Codex protocol was generated locally without account requests: ordinaryUsageAllowed null is explicitly unavailable; thread status `idle` is supported along with legacy `inactive`.
- Validation: typecheck/compile/diff whitespace PASS; full intermediate suite PASS (96/96), including cross-window independent-cache, corrupted storage, lifecycle, short-lock, and delayed-dispatch regressions.
